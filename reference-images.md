## Optional: prompts for fresh hero imagery

You don't need new images — the screenshots are strong. But if you want a custom
hero illustration instead of (or behind) a product shot, here are on-brand
generation prompts. Keep any generated art subordinate to the product UI.

**Option A — Naturalist's specimen tray (most on-brand)**
> A flat-lay illustration in the style of a vintage naturalist's specimen display
> case: a deep forest-green felt-lined tray, with neatly pinned and labeled "specimens"
> that are actually small job-listing cards and a fine line-drawing of a hand net.
> Warm cream parchment label tags with a single mint-green accent tag. Muted,
> museum-archive palette (#2C5F4A forest, #FDFAF6 cream, #7DD4A8 mint). Soft daylight,
> meticulous, calm, premium. No text, no UI, no neon, no gradients.

**Option B — The net, abstracted**
> A minimal line illustration of a hand net gently catching a single falling paper
> card, drawn in fine cream strokes on a deep forest-green background. Lots of negative
> space, one small mint-green accent dot like a specimen tag. Calm, editorial, quiet.
> No text, no glow, no 3D render.

**Option C — Soft background texture only**
> A subtle parchment / vellum paper texture in warm cream (#FDFAF6) with the faintest
> botanical archive watermark, very low contrast, meant to sit behind text. No focal
> subject, no color beyond cream and the faintest forest-green linework.

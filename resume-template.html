What makes this resume template ATS-friendly?

It's one column of real text with standard section headings and real bullet points, so the software employers use to scan resumes reads everything in the right order.

No tables, text boxes, columns, or images, and your contact details sit in the page itself rather than the header area, where some systems never look. Dates are written the same way for every job, month and year.

How do I write strong bullet points?

Say what you did, the situation you did it in, and the measurable result, in one or two lines.

  • Lead with a verb that says what you did."Responsible for onboarding" becomes "Led a redesign of self-serve onboarding."
  • Add the result, with a number you could defend in an interview."Improved conversion" becomes "Raised trial-to-paid conversion from 9% to 13% in two quarters."
  • Show the judgment, not just the activity."Managed the roadmap" becomes "Cut the roadmap from 14 initiatives to 5 after 60 customer interviews traced churn to reporting gaps."
  • One achievement per bullet, and name your tools exactly.Recruiters search for tools by name, so list them in Skills as written: Jira, Figma, Amplitude, SQL.

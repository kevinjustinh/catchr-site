# Catchr — Brand Guide

> Portable distillation of Catchr's visual identity for use when designing the
> landing page. Hand this to Claude Design alongside the prompt.

---

## 1. What Catchr is

Catchr is a Chrome extension that turns job hunting from a chaotic, anxious
process into a calm, ordered one. You catch any job listing in one click — it's
logged to your own Google Sheet — and then three AI tools help you act on it:

- **Check Fit** — an instant AI score of how well your background matches the role.
- **Tailor Resume** — surgical, no-hallucination rewrites of your resume bullets to match the job.
- **Interview Prep** — a role-specific prep doc with likely questions and talking points drawn from your resume.

The AI features are the headline. One-click catching is the effortless on-ramp.

---

## 2. Design philosophy — "Verdant Archive"

A philosophy rooted in the act of collection — the quiet discipline of the
naturalist who catalogues what others let pass. The world is noise; the archive
is signal. Beauty lives not in chaotic abundance but in the patient work of
selecting, preserving, and ordering. Every element earns its place through
reduction. What remains is essential. What remains is enough.

Practical translation for the landing page:

- **Color speaks before type does.** A deep forest interior anchors the page with
  the weight of something grown over time. Pale warm cream is not empty space —
  it is material surface: parchment, vellum, the page as artifact. The mint accent
  reads like a specimen tag on a pinned wing — precise, informational, used once.
- **Generous, deliberate spacing** — the air of a museum display case. Enough room
  to see each thing clearly; never so much that context is lost.
- **Typography used the way a museum uses text** — sparingly, precisely, always in
  service of the thing being shown. One headline as a singular declaration.
- **Inevitability.** The composition should feel effortless and final, as though it
  could not have been otherwise. No decoration for decoration's sake.

Emotional tone: **calm, confident, meticulous, quietly premium.** The opposite of
hustle-culture urgency. Catchr makes an anxious task feel handled.

## 3. Color palette

### Core
| Role | Hex | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| Forest (primary) | `#2C5F4A` | Hero backgrounds, nav, primary buttons, the brand's anchor |
| Forest deep | `#245040` | Gradients, pressed states, footer |
| Cream (surface) | `#FDFAF6` | Page background, cards — the "parchment" |
| Cream warm | `#EDE8E2` | Secondary surfaces, dividers, input fills |
| Ink | `#2C2C2A` | Body text on cream |
| Stone / taupe | `#8C7E6E` | Eyebrow labels, captions, secondary text |
| Stone light | `#B0A090` | Tertiary text, muted detail |

### Accent — use sparingly
| Role | Hex | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| Mint (specimen tag) | `#7DD4A8` | The single live accent — highlights, "Strong Match", small flourishes |
| Mint soft | `#B8E0CC` | Progress fills, subtle tints |
| Mint wash | `#EAF7F1` | Faint background tint for callouts |

### Feature accents (match the product UI exactly)
| Feature | Hex | Use |
|---------|-----|-----|
| Check Fit | `#7A8C5A` (sage) | Section accent / icon for the Check Fit feature |
| Tailor Resume | `#8C5E72` (mauve) | Section accent / icon for the Tailor Resume feature |
| Interview Prep | `#4A6580` (slate) | Section accent / icon for the Interview Prep feature |

### Functional
| Role | Hex |
|------|-----|
| Alert / error | `#C0392B` |
| Pure white (rare) | `#FFFFFF` |

**Rule:** Forest + cream do 90% of the work. Mint is the one bright note — treat it
like a single specimen tag, never a theme. Feature accents appear only within
their own feature's context.

## 4. Typography

- **Display / headlines:** **Bricolage Grotesque** — weighted, confident, slightly
  characterful. Use heavy weights (700) for hero and section headlines. Tight
  tracking, tight leading on multi-line headlines.
- **Body / UI:** **Work Sans** — quiet authority, excellent at small sizes. 400 for
  body, 500–600 for labels and buttons.
- **Eyebrow labels:** Work Sans, uppercase, letter-spaced (~0.08em), stone color,
  small. e.g. `CATCHR FOR CHROME`.

Both load from Google Fonts. Never substitute Inter, Roboto, Arial, or system-ui.

Hierarchy: one dominant headline per section. Body copy appears only where the
visual can't carry the load alone.

---

## 5. Form & layout

- **Cards / surfaces:** generous corner radius (~16–20px), cream fill, very soft
  shadow. The product popup itself uses a rounded, floating card — echo that.
- **Buttons (primary):** forest fill, cream text, comfortable padding, subtle depth.
- **Buttons (secondary):** outlined in forest or stone, transparent fill.
- **Geometry through reduction:** only what must be rectangular is; only what must
  curve does. No decorative shapes.
- **Spacing:** 8px rhythm. Lean toward more breathing room than feels necessary —
  the display-case principle.
- **Imagery:** the product is shown as a floating popup card against a solid color
  field (often forest or a feature accent). Let the screenshots sit in calm,
  uncluttered space.

---

## 6. Logo

The mark is a hand net / catch-basket ("catchr 🎣") rendered as a fine line drawing.
Variants provided: light-on-cream, light-on-forest, and an inverted outline. Pair
with the "Catchr" wordmark in Bricolage Grotesque. On dark/forest backgrounds use
the cream version of the mark.

---

## 7. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Lead with color and space; let one headline carry each section.
- Keep the mint accent rare and intentional.
- Match feature accent colors to the product UI (sage / mauve / slate).
- Make it feel inevitable, calm, and premium.

**Don't**
- No generic-AI gradients, neon glow, or dark-mode "tech" aesthetic.
- No raw hex outside this palette.
- No crowding — resist filling whitespace.
- No urgency/hype copy. Confidence, not pressure.

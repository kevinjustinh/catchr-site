What's in the template?

Twelve columns that cover a job from the posting to the offer, plus a Dashboard tab with your response rate, interviews, offers, and the applications to follow up on.

Keep a row for every job you're considering, not only the ones you've applied to. It's how you spot patterns later: which roles reply, and how long they take. Every column has a filter button, so you can show just one company, one outcome, or only the jobs you've applied to.

How do I open it in Google Sheets?

Click Open in Google Sheets, then Make a copy. Your own copy lands in your Google Drive, with the dropdowns, colors, and formulas ready to use.

  1. aClick Open in Google Sheets and sign in to Google if it asks.
  2. bClick Make a copy. The copy is yours, saved in your own Google Drive.
  3. cDelete the three example rows and add your first job.

Prefer Excel? Download the .xlsx file instead. It opens in Excel, and you can upload it to Google Drive later.

When should I follow up on an application?

If a week has passed with no reply, a short, polite follow-up is reasonable. The Dashboard tab's Follow-Up Zone shows up to six applications that are 7 to 14 days old with no response, so you know when it's time.

After 14 days with no reply, the application moves to the Ghosting Watchlist. That's not a rule, just a sign it may be time to put your energy into newer applications.

Can the tracker fill itself in?

Yes. Catchr is a free Chrome extension that adds the job to your Google Sheet in one click, using these same columns, from LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, Wellfound, and Ashby.

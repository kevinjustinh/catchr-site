Setup guide

Up and running in minutes.

Catchr needs three things to work: a free Gemini API key, your resume in Google Docs, and a Google account connection. Here's exactly how to do each one.

About 5 minutes total
01

Required

Get a free Gemini API key

Catchr's AI runs on Google's Gemini model using your own key. The free tier has daily limits, and you won't be charged unless you turn on billing yourself.

  1. a Go to Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.
  2. b Click Get API key in the left sidebar.
  3. c Click Create API key and select or create a Google Cloud project when prompted. (If you don't have a project yet, just click "Create new project". It takes one second.)
  4. d Copy the key that appears. Then paste it into the Gemini API Key field in the Catchr extension. It saves automatically.
Gemini's free tier has daily limits. If you hit one, the AI tools work again once it resets. Catchr removes your name and contact details from your resume before it is sent. On the free tier, Google may use the rest (like your work history and skills) to improve its AI, and its reviewers may read it. Turning on billing for your key's project in Google AI Studio stops Google using it to improve its AI, though Google may still keep requests for a limited time to check for misuse. Your key is saved in your browser and sent only to Google's Gemini API, never to us. In the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland, or the UK, Google allows only paid Gemini access, so turn on billing for your project in Google AI Studio before you use the key.
02

Required for AI features

Add your resume

Catchr reads your resume directly from Google Docs using your own account. It only accesses the file you choose and the files it creates for you, like tailored copies.

  1. a Make sure your resume is in Google Docs. If it's currently a Word file or PDF, upload it to Google Drive and open it with Google Docs first.
  2. b Open the Catchr extension and go to ⚙ Settings. In the Resumes section, click Add a resume.
  3. c A Google file picker will open. Find and select your resume. Catchr only gets access to the file you pick.
  4. d Give it a short label, for example Product Manager or Software Engineer. It saves when you click away.
  5. e You can add multiple resumes if you're targeting different types of roles. Catchr will ask which one to use whenever it tailors.
Catchr never stores your resume on a server of its own. It reads it from your Google Docs when you use an AI tool, and only the file you select is ever accessed. Before your resume goes to the AI, Catchr removes your name and contact details.
03

Required for logging & saving

Connect your Google account

Catchr needs Google account access to read your resume, log jobs to your Sheet, and save generated documents back to your Drive. You only do this once.

  1. a The first time you use a Catchr feature that requires Drive or Sheets access, a Google sign-in prompt will appear automatically.
  2. b Sign in and grant the requested permissions. Catchr uses these only to read the resume file you picked, write rows to your job tracker Sheet, and save tailored resumes and prep docs to your Drive.
Catchr only accesses the specific files it creates or that you explicitly point it to. Nothing else in your Google account is read or modified.
04

Optional

Bring your own job tracker sheet

By default Catchr creates a ready-to-use Google Sheet for you automatically when you catch your first job. If you already have a sheet you'd like to use, you can connect it instead.

  1. a Open ⚙ Settings in the extension, click Connect next to Tracker sheet, and choose your Google Sheet.
  2. b Row 1 of your sheet must have column headers. Catchr will match Company, Position, Link, Applied?, Date Applied, and Outcome by name, in any capitalization. Any extra columns are left untouched.
  3. c Skip this step and Catchr will create and name a sheet for you when you catch your first job.
  4. d Starting a sheet from scratch? Our free job tracker template already uses Catchr's columns.
